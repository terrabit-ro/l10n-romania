Separate numbering of the documents of Romanian cash journals.

- Each cash payment gets a cash document type, and each type has its own
  sequence on the cash journal: customer receipts (``CH``), supplier
  payments (``PL``), payment orders (``DP``), collection orders (``DI``)
  and internal transfers (``IT``), instead of the journal's single
  payment sequence.
- On cash journals, the payment methods post directly on the cash
  account of the journal (no outstanding receipts/payments account), so
  a cash payment moves the cash account as soon as it is posted. When a
  cash journal is created or its account or payment methods change, the
  payment methods are aligned and the journal's existing lines on the
  old outstanding account are moved to the cash account.
