1. Go to *Invoicing > Accounting > Cash Register* and create (or let the scheduled action create)
   the register of a cash journal for a day.
2. Use *Add Receipt*, *Add Payment* or *Operation* to record the day's cash movements; the
   register lists the posted entries on the cash account and computes the balances.
3. Print the register with *Print*.
4. At the end of the day press *Close Day*. The balance at closing, who closed the day and when
   are recorded on the register.
5. To correct a closed day, press *Reopen* (the later closed days of the same cash journal are
   reopened too), or record a reversal dated today.
