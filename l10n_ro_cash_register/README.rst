Romanian cash register (*Registru de casă*, form 14-4-7A of OMFP
2634/2015) as a numbered document, one per cash journal and day.

- the register is numbered with its own sequence, unique per journal and
  date;
- the opening balance is carried over automatically from the previous
  day and the closing balance is computed from the posted entries on the
  cash account of the journal (``default_account_id``, e.g. 5311);
- the day's movements are listed with receipts, payments, the
  explanation and the partner;
- the register prints in the layout of the form;
- **daily closing**: closing a day freezes its balance, and no entry on
  the cash account dated on that day or earlier can be posted, reset to
  draft or deleted until the register is reopened. Reopening a day also
  reopens the later closed days of the same cash journal, because the
  balance is carried forward from day to day. A day in the future or
  with a negative balance cannot be closed. Corrections for a closed day
  are made with a reversal or a correction entry dated today.

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Invoicing > Accounting > Cash Register* and create (or let the
   scheduled action create) the register of a cash journal for a day.
2. Use *Add Receipt*, *Add Payment* or *Operation* to record the day's
   cash movements; the register lists the posted entries on the cash
   account and computes the balances.
3. Print the register with *Print*.
4. At the end of the day press *Close Day*. The balance at closing, who
   closed the day and when are recorded on the register.
5. To correct a closed day, press *Reopen* (the later closed days of the
   same cash journal are reopened too), or record a reversal dated
   today.
